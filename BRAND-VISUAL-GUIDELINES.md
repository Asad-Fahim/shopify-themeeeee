# Nasir Book Depot - Visual Style Guidelines

## Overall Visual Direction
**Status**: ✅ APPROVED

The visual direction establishes Nasir Book Depot as a professional, trustworthy bookstore with a warm, literary aesthetic. The design balances modern functionality with traditional bookstore values, emphasizing readability, cultural richness, and a premium shopping experience.

---

## 1. Icon Style Guidelines

### Technical Specifications
- **Stroke Width**: 2px (consistent across all icons)
- **Style**: Outline/stroke-based icons (not filled)
- **Corner Radius**: Sharp edges (0px border radius)
- **Line Cap**: Round
- **Line Join**: Round
- **Size**: 18px standard, 22px for header icons, scalable

### Icon Categories & Usage

#### Navigation Icons
- **Header Hamburger**: 3-line menu icon
- **Chevron**: Directional arrows for dropdowns and accordions
- **Search**: Magnifying glass for search functionality
- **Close**: X icon for modals and drawers

#### E-commerce Icons
- **Shopping Bag**: Primary cart icon (default)
- **Shopping Cart**: Alternative cart icon
- **Tote Bag**: Alternative cart icon
- **Heart**: Wishlist functionality (if implemented)

#### Account & Service Icons
- **Customer/Person**: Account and login
- **Email/Envelope**: Newsletter and contact
- **Filters**: Collection filtering
- **Share**: Social sharing on product pages

#### Feedback Icons
- **Check/Success**: Form success states
- **Error/Cross**: Form error states
- **Info**: Informational tooltips

### Color Application
- **Primary Icons**: Header text color (#FAF7F0 on dark backgrounds)
- **Secondary Icons**: Text color (#1F2933 on light backgrounds)
- **Accent Icons**: Golden accent (#C49A45) for highlights
- **Hover States**: 10% opacity increase or color shift to primary green

### Animation Guidelines
- **Hover**: Subtle scale (1.05x) or opacity change
- **Loading**: Gentle rotation for processing states
- **Transitions**: 0.2s ease-in-out timing
- **No bounce effects**: Maintain professional tone

## 2. Photography Style Guidelines

### Overall Aesthetic
- **Mood**: Warm, inviting, intellectual, culturally rich
- **Lighting**: Soft, natural light with subtle shadows
- **Color Temperature**: Warm (3000-4000K) to complement cream backgrounds
- **Depth of Field**: Moderate, keeping subjects sharp with gentle background blur

### Subject Matter

#### Book Photography
- **Cover Shots**: Clean, front-facing book covers with minimal props
- **Lifestyle**: Books in reading environments (desks, shelves, cozy corners)
- **Detail Shots**: Close-ups of textures, spines, page edges
- **Stacking**: Artful book stacks showing variety and depth

#### Environmental Photography
- **Store Interiors**: Warm, well-lit bookstore spaces
- **Reading Spaces**: Cozy nooks, comfortable seating areas
- **Cultural Elements**: Subtle nods to Urdu, Hindi, and English literary traditions
- **Community**: People reading, browsing, engaging with books (if applicable)

### Technical Standards
- **Resolution**: Minimum 2400px width for hero images
- **Format**: JPG for photographs, PNG for graphics with transparency
- **Color Profile**: sRGB
- **Compression**: High quality (80-90% in Photoshop)

### Composition Rules
- **Rule of Thirds**: Apply for dynamic compositions
- **Negative Space**: Generous breathing room around subjects
- **Leading Lines**: Guide eye toward focal points
- **Symmetry**: Use for formal, authoritative shots

### Color Grading
- **Base**: Natural, slightly warm tones
- **Contrast**: Moderate, avoid harsh blacks
- **Saturation**: Slightly desaturated for sophisticated look
- **Consistency**: Apply preset or LUT for brand consistency

## 3. Product Image Standards

### Image Specifications

#### Primary Product Images
- **Dimensions**: 2048 x 2048px (1:1 square ratio)
- **File Size**: Under 500KB per image
- **Background**: Pure white (#FFFFFF) or consistent neutral
- **Resolution**: 72 DPI (web standard)
- **Format**: PNG with transparency or JPG with white background

#### Secondary/Angled Views
- **Dimensions**: 2048 x 2048px
- **Angles**: Front cover, spine, back cover, 3/4 view, open book spread
- **Consistency**: Same lighting and background across all views

#### Lifestyle/Context Images
- **Dimensions**: 2048 x 1365px (3:2 ratio) or 2048 x 2048px
- **Context**: Books in realistic reading environments
- **Props**: Minimal, culturally relevant items (reading glasses, bookmarks, tea)

### Photography Setup

#### Lighting
- **Main Light**: Softbox at 45-degree angle
- **Fill Light**: Reflector or secondary soft light
- **Back Light**: Subtle rim light for separation
- **Shadows**: Soft, diffuse shadows

#### Background Options
- **Studio White**: Clean, professional e-commerce standard
- **Wooden Surface**: Warm, natural texture (for lifestyle)
- **Bookshelf Context**: In-situ photography (for featured products)

#### Styling Guidelines
- **Clean Covers**: Dust-free, pristine condition
- **Stand Upright**: Books positioned naturally, not forced
- **Group Shots**: 2-4 books maximum, arranged harmoniously
- **Props**: Minimal - bookmark, reading glasses, simple objects

### File Naming Convention
```
[ISBN]-[view]-[number].jpg
Examples:
978-0-123456-47-2-front-01.jpg
978-0-123456-47-2-spine-01.jpg
978-0-123456-47-2-lifestyle-01.jpg
```

### Alt Text Standards
- **Format**: "[Book Title] by [Author], [cover description]"
- **Example**: "The Alchemist by Paulo Coelho, paperback edition with blue cover"
- **Length**: Under 125 characters for SEO

## 4. Banner Image Standards

### Hero Slideshow Banners

#### Dimensions
- **Desktop**: 2160 x 1080px (2:1 ratio)
- **Tablet**: 1536 x 768px (2:1 ratio)
- **Mobile**: 1000 x 1400px (5:7 ratio) - separate mobile image recommended

#### Technical Requirements
- **File Size**: Under 800KB for desktop, under 400KB for mobile
- **Format**: JPG (photography) or PNG (graphics)
- **Color Space**: sRGB
- **Quality**: 85% compression

#### Content Guidelines

##### Composition
- **Text Area**: Safe zone - center 60% of image
- **Focal Point**: Center or left-third for text overlay
- **Depth**: Layered composition with foreground/background interest
- **Subject**: Books, reading scenes, or abstract literary motifs

##### Typography Integration
- **Headlines**: 48-72px on desktop, 24-36px on mobile
- **Subheadlines**: 24-36px on desktop, 16-20px on mobile
- **Text Color**: White (#FFFFFF) with dark overlay or brand green (#1F4D3A)
- **Overlay**: 30% black opacity for text readability
- **Font**: DM Sans Bold for headlines, Regular for body

##### Color Schemes
- **Option 1**: Warm cream background with dark text
- **Option 2**: Deep green background with light text
- **Option 3**: Dark gray with golden accents
- **Option 4**: Lifestyle photography with brand color overlay

#### Animation & Transition
- **Type**: Reveal or Fade (preferred over Sweep)
- **Duration**: 5 seconds per slide
- **Autoplay**: Enabled
- **Progress Indicator**: Visible dots

### Collection Banner Images

#### Dimensions
- **Desktop**: 1200 x 600px (2:1 ratio)
- **Mobile**: 800 x 800px (1:1 ratio)

#### Content Strategy
- **Urdu Books**: Incorporate Urdu typography or cultural elements
- **Hindi Books**: Include Dev Nagari script or Indian motifs
- **English Books**: Classic Western literary imagery
- **All Books**: Diverse, inclusive representation

### Promotional Block Images

#### Dimensions
- **Single Block**: 2400 x 800px
- **Double Block**: 1200 x 600px each

#### Usage Context
- **Seasonal Promotions**: Holiday sales, new arrivals
- **Collection Features**: Highlighting specific genres
